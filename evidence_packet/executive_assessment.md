# Executive Assessment

MITRA now has one user-facing execution coordinator. Companion and frontend
workflow requests both enter the strict ecosystem runtime, which invokes Raj,
the selected product, conditional KESHAV, Ashmit, Bucket, Karma, PRANA,
InsightFlow, replay, and Central Depository through published contracts.

The architectural acceptance target is implemented and covered by controlled
interoperability tests. Production certification remains conditional on the
external blockers recorded in `DEP/blockers.md`; they are not represented as
passes.
